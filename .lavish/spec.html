🏗️ Architecture

One Zod Schema SSoT

CampaignConfigSchema drives LLM output, form, DB, and publish validation.

Chat & Form → One Store

AI streams patches; form edits directly. Changes sync bidirectionally.

Deterministic Where Possible

Budget & eligibility = code. LLM = understanding + copy.

Mock LLM Must Always Work

Offline canned responses keep the demo alive.

📊 Budget / Reach / ROI Simulator

eligibleCount
COUNT(*) with eligibility WHERE
acceptanceRate
nano 45% → mega 5%
estimatedCost
flat fees + product + commission + reserve
estimatedReach
Σ followers × (views/followers) × 0.4
estimatedCpa
cost / (reach × 0.008)
estimatedRoi
(Gmv × 0.15 − cost) / cost

Returns top 12 eligible creators by GMV. All math is deterministic pure functions — testable without LLM or DB mocks.
