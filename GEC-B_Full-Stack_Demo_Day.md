<callout emoji="💡">
**选题建议**
- 先想清楚：你在解决什么问题？这个问题是否是真实问题，为什么值得做？在当前的业务中，有没有你觉得重要/有意思的项目，可以通过全栈承接，并且在全栈中能有更多的价值;
- 优先选择你熟悉、且能在 2–3 周内做出清晰闭环的业务方向;
- 一个聚焦的小产品，往往比一个铺得很大的想法更容易打动评审。
**选题示例**
**项目：TSP服务商工作台 - 增加过期提醒组件与快捷登录入口**
- 解决的真实问题（聚焦小切口）：通过一个小小的“提醒Widget”和“快捷入口”，就直接替代了原本需要人工用Excel和飞书机器人维护的繁琐流程，挽回了潜在的GMV流失和客户流失。
- 全栈承接的独特价值： 需求虽小，但底层涉及多市场/多角色的复杂权限交叉。全栈承接免去了前后端反复对齐的沟通成本，且能从全局视角直接复用和微调现有接口（GORM/ES），避免重复开发，快速拿到了清晰的业务闭环。

1. **One Person Team only.** Team formation is not allowed. Each participant must complete the project independently.
2. **Open to all participants in the GEC-B full-stack training program.**

<callout emoji="💡">
**How to choose a strong topic**
- Start by getting clear on this: what problem are you solving? Is it a real problem, and why is it worth doing? In your current business context, is there an important or interesting project that could be carried forward through a full-stack approach—and create even more value because it is built end to end?
- Prefer a direction you understand well and can close end-to-end within 2–3 weeks
- A focused small product is often more compelling than a broad but unfinished concept
**Topic example**  
**Project: TSP workbench — adding an "expiration reminder" widget and a "quick login" entry.**
- The real problem it solves (a focused, small entry point): a small "reminder widget" and "quick login" entry directly replace the tedious manual process that previously relied on Excel and a Lark bot, recovering potential GMV loss and customer churn.
- The unique value of a full-stack approach: although the requirement is small, at the underlying level it involves complex permission intersections across multiple markets and roles. A full-stack approach removes the repeated frontend-backend alignment cost, and lets you reuse and fine-tune existing interfaces (GORM/ES) from a global perspective, avoiding duplicate development and quickly delivering a clear business loop.
</callout>
